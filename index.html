      Purpose and instructions
      1. Explore all 17 profiles in the AI Facility Stakeholder Forum. Assess influence and interest from 1 to 5, and identify each stakeholder’s position. Select Unknown / insufficient evidence when appropriate.
      2. Influence describes the ability to affect decisions, approvals, resources, or project delivery. Interest describes how directly the project affects the stakeholder’s responsibilities, interests, or community. Scores may repeat.
      3. The app calculates influence × interest as an engagement-priority score. A high score can apply to a supporter or to a concerned stakeholder. Use it as a planning aid.
      4. Select three distinct stakeholders with the greatest potential to delay, constrain, or require changes to your project. Priority 1 is your greatest potential barrier; priorities 2 and 3 follow. A stakeholder can occupy only one priority.
      5. For each selection, explain the concern, potential barrier, supporting evidence and assumptions, and an engagement action or project change that addresses the concern.
      6. Identify which of your top three you would engage first and explain why. Review your responses, then use Print / Save PDF. Submit the saved PDF as directed in your course.

      1. Assess all 17 stakeholders

      Score influence and interest from 1 = very low to 5 = very high. Choose a position based on the profiles and your selected state and project. All three fields are required for each stakeholder.

      The forum includes state, federal, Tribal Nation, industry, environmental, labor, infrastructure, and local representatives. Assess each role using its profile and your project context.

      Engagement priority = Influence × Interest (1–25). This score helps you plan engagement. Assess the stakeholder’s position and the specific potential barrier separately.

      2. Choose your top three potential barriers

      Choose three different stakeholders. Priority 1 is the greatest potential barrier. Your choices should reflect specific concerns and project evidence. They are not automatically selected from the engagement scores.

      If a stakeholder is already chosen, change or clear that earlier choice before using the stakeholder in another priority.

      3. Plan your first contact

      Which of your top three stakeholders would you engage first, and why? Consider timing, decisions, dependencies, and the concern you need to address.
